# Agent Instructions

- Preserve the project's browser-first Three.js architecture unless a measured technical reason justifies a change.
- Work in small, reviewable tasks. Do not claim a feature is complete until its acceptance checks have been run.
- Keep desktop and touch controls in scope; do not silently drop mobile support.
- Distinguish static checks, automated behavior checks, browser rendering checks, and real-device playtests.
- Do not introduce unlicensed third-party models, textures, fonts, audio, or code.
- Keep the game fictional and stylized. Do not add real-world weapon construction or operational guidance.
